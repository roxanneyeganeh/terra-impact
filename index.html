    Our mission

    We believe stopping plastic pollution starts with clear and measurable action.

    Read our story

    At Terra, we’re on a mission to stop plastic pollution at its source. Every product is designed to make a measurable environmental impact.

    For every 10 bottles sold, we aim to fund the collection of 11.4 kg of ocean-bound plastic, the equivalent of 1,000 plastic bottles.

    How it works

    Data-Driven Design

    This design pattern is intended to creatively visualise your personal habits, making the environmental impact of everyday choices such as transport, recycling, and energy use more visible and meaningful.

    By translating your responses into colour and form, the pattern encourages thoughtful reflection on your connection to the environment. It is more than just a visually appealing design; it is a tool for raising awareness and supporting mindful, sustainable behaviour.
