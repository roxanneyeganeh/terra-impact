Terra’s goal is to accelerate climate awareness through personalised, data-driven products that reflect your environmental impact.
An experience that turns your choices into visuals. Highlighting the tangible difference individuals can make by bringing visibility to the often unseen effects of their actions.

This design-driven experience fosters eco-consciousness and encourages a more responsible relationship with the environment.
The average person uses 167 plastic bottles annually, many of which end up in landfills or oceans. Plastic bottles can take up to 450 years to decompose, polluting soil and water for centuries. Globally, plastic production is expected to double by 2040, making reuse and reduction more urgent than ever.

Our Mission

We believe stopping plastic pollution starts with clear and measurable action.

At Terra, we’re on a mission to stop plastic pollution at its source. Every product is designed to make a measurable environmental impact.
