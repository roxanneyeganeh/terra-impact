Terra is focused on preventing plastic pollution by creating products that drive real environmental change.

Our approach combines innovative design with measurable impact to help protect ecosystems and reduce plastic waste worldwide.

Our Goal

Remove billions of kilograms of plastic from the environment through collective action.
Fund the removal of over 11 kilograms of ocean-bound plastic with every product sold.
Support thousands of plastic collectors through community-based programs.
Improve waste infrastructure in hundreds of underserved communities.

Designed for Circularity

Terra products are made with 67% recycled materials. Every design is modular, repairable, and recyclable. Built for longevity and minimal footprint.

Track Your Impact

Our refill and reuse tracking platform enables users to see the real-time effect of their actions. Every refill contributes to collecting plastic waste from vulnerable regions.

Built on Values

Terra is committed to full transparency and traceability. We publish detailed impact reports and hold ourselves to the highest environmental and social standards.
